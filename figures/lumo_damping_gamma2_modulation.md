# LUMO — Does SAR see damping indirectly? ζ vs γ² vs sub-aperture modulation
New: modal damping ratio ζ (half-power bandwidth, raw Uni-Hannover SHM recordings, `lumo_damping_ratio.json`, state-adaptive mode windows, SNR gate). Existing: whole-tower γ² and intra-dwell brightness modulation from the same Sentinel-1 stack — one row per distinct overpass (acquisition minute + orbit, VV preferred; the artifact's burst rows are de-duplicated); SHM wind-response curves (`wind_response_shm.json`).

Three damage states (DAM3, DAM4, DAM6) are compared, each against its OWN healthy campaign (`campaign_layout`: 05_Healthy / 03_Healthy / 01_Healthy). Those healthy ζ(H1) baselines differ by ~2e-4, so panel 1 of the figure plots **Δζ** rather than absolute ζ — an absolute-ζ line would show a healthy "trend" that is pure baseline drift between campaigns. DAM4 has no SHM wind-response recordings (`wind_response_shm.json` covers dam3 + dam6 only), so its wind column is `open` by construction.

## Mechanism 1 — γ² as a damping proxy
* dam3: ζ(H1) -0.0008, γ² +0.0118 → damping-driven consistency: **True**.
* dam4: ζ(H1) -0.0008, γ² -0.0035 → damping-driven consistency: **False**.
* dam6: ζ(H1) -0.0011, γ² -0.0091 → damping-driven consistency: **False**.
* Interpretation: γ² is the NET vibration channel. DAM6 γ² drops sharply (stiffness loss dominates); DAM3 γ² is slightly UP (suppression); DAM4 sits between them, DOWN but only at ~40 % of DAM6's Δ (and with the largest median `n_masked`, 14 vs DAM6's 12 — see `lumo_gamma2_confound_diagnosis.md`). A pure damping proxy would predict γ² UP in every state, because ζ(H1) falls in all three — only DAM3 is consistent. **γ² alone is NOT a damping proxy; it is a vibration-amplitude channel.**

## Mechanism 2 — sub-aperture modulation as damping/wind-response sensor
* dam3: modulation -0.0307 vs SHM wind-resp ratio 0.3782 → modulation tracks the SHM amplitude: **True**.
* dam4: modulation +0.0312 vs SHM wind-resp ratio open → modulation tracks the SHM amplitude: **False**.
* dam6: modulation +0.0132 vs SHM wind-resp ratio 2.0473 → modulation tracks the SHM amplitude: **True**.
* `open` means there are NO SHM wind-response recordings for that campaign (true of DAM4), so its `tracks` flag is False by construction — absence of evidence, not evidence against the wind-response mapping.
* Interpretation: DAM3's SHM suppression (ratio 0.3782) maps onto shallower modulation (0.8213, p=0.088) and DAM6's higher wind-normalized response (2.0473) onto deeper modulation (0.8652, p=0.134) — the two states whose SHM amplitude is measured both agree in sign. DAM4 shows the DEEPEST modulation of the three (0.8832, p=0.047, the only p < 0.05) but has no wind-response recordings, so its amplitude is unknown and that leg cannot be closed for it. **The sub-aperture modulation tracks the wind-normalized vibration amplitude, which is the net of damping + stiffness.**

## DAM4 — SAR-weak, damping-strong
* **ζ (SHM)**: H1 0.00328 → 0.00245 (Δ -0.00083, δ -1.00) — the lowest damaged H1 median and complete separation, i.e. the *relatively* largest H1 damping loss of the three states; fundamental 0.01490 → 0.01655 (Δ +0.00165, δ +0.84) — the largest fundamental ζ increase. So on the SHM side DAM4 is not a mild case.
* **γ² (SAR)**: 0.0453 → 0.0417 (Δ -0.0035, Welch p=0.714) — the weakest contrast of the three states (which in the committed `LUMO_GAMMA2_MASKED` units stand at +0.1392 / -0.0527 / -0.1281 for DAM3 / DAM4 / DAM6) *and* the least separable from the N/brightness bookkeeping: the N-mechanism surrogate (band a, `lumo_gamma2_confound_diagnosis.md`) puts its raw δ at percentile +12.75 (inside the band, vs +51.05 for DAM3 and +4.5 for DAM6, which is the only state outside it). 87.5 % (14/16) of its rows lie inside the healthy N/brightness box — the best-supported positivity of the three (DAM3 84.0 %, DAM6 50.0 %) — with a median `n_masked` of 14 against the healthy 15, so its raw γ² deficit is what the healthy γ² = -0.0090 + 1.3886/N law already predicts.
* **N controls**: fixed-N (k = 10) δ = -0.2295, between DAM3 (-0.1096) and DAM6 (-0.3375); but the closest-N matched control flips its sign to +0.0306 on 14 pairs (DAM3 -0.0350 on 20, DAM6 -0.2200 on 10). It is the only state with the same number of overpasses in each season (8 summer, 8 winter), so this contrast is not a season artifact — unlike DAM3, whose contrast is summer-only and whose sign flips with orbit.
* **Modulation (SAR)**: 0.852 → 0.883 (Δ +0.0312, p=0.047) — the DEEPEST modulation of the three and the only state reaching p < 0.05. With no wind-response recordings for `04_DAM4_111` there is no common-wind amplitude to explain it, so this is the one channel where DAM4 leads and the one that cannot yet be closed.
* **Sampling**: 16 SAR overpasses (8 summer Mar-Jul, 8 winter Oct-Feb), like DAM6 and unlike DAM3 (25, summer only); ζ from 5 SHM recordings per campaign. Net: DAM4 is the state where the two SAR channels disagree — deepest modulation, weakest and least separable γ² — which is the signature of a response channel dominated by amplitude/mask bookkeeping rather than by ζ.

## Verdict
* **dam3** (`05_Healthy` → `06_DAM3_111`): ζ(H1) 0.00337 → 0.00257 (Δ -0.00080, Cliff's δ -0.92); ζ(fund) Δ -0.00034 (δ +0.20); γ² 0.0453 → 0.0570 (Welch p=0.9091); modulation 0.852 → 0.821 (p=0.088); SHM wind-resp ratio @4 m/s 0.3782.
* **dam4** (`03_Healthy` → `04_DAM4_111`): ζ(H1) 0.00328 → 0.00245 (Δ -0.00083, Cliff's δ -1.00); ζ(fund) Δ +0.00165 (δ +0.84); γ² 0.0453 → 0.0417 (Welch p=0.7135); modulation 0.852 → 0.883 (p=0.047); SHM wind-resp ratio @4 m/s open (no SHM wind recordings for this campaign).
* **dam6** (`01_Healthy` → `02_DAM6_111`): ζ(H1) 0.00403 → 0.00290 (Δ -0.00113, Cliff's δ -1.00); ζ(fund) Δ +0.00133 (δ +0.28); γ² 0.0453 → 0.0362 (Welch p=0.0787); modulation 0.852 → 0.865 (p=0.134); SHM wind-resp ratio @4 m/s 2.0473.
* **Direct damping evidence is limited**: the half-power ζ of the 1st higher mode (H1) DECREASES in ALL THREE damaged states — dam3 0.00337→0.00257 (Δ -0.00080, δ -0.92), dam4 0.00328→0.00245 (Δ -0.00083, δ -1.00), dam6 0.00403→0.00290 (Δ -0.00113, δ -1.00) — i.e. narrower PSD peaks in the damaged recordings, at a similar relative loss in DAM3/DAM4 and a somewhat larger one in DAM6 even though the three states differ strongly in stiffness (fundamental mode: dam3 Δ -0.00034 (δ +0.20), dam4 Δ +0.00165 (δ +0.84), dam6 Δ +0.00133 (δ +0.28)). Under ambient wind excitation the half-power width is biased by the excitation spectrum, and the SHM shows a strongly SUPPRESSED wind response in DAM3, so the apparent ζ decrease may be excitation-driven rather than a true damping change — and it cannot rank damage severity either, because the state with the lowest damaged ζ(H1) (DAM4) is also the one with the weakest and least separable SAR response.
* **SAR sees the VIBRATION RESPONSE, not ζ itself**: γ² (net amplitude) and sub-aperture modulation separate the states in the direction predicted by the SHM amplitude at common wind wherever that amplitude is measured — DAM3 suppressed (ratio 0.38) → shallower modulation, DAM6 amplified (2.05) → deeper modulation plus the only γ² drop that survives a fixed-N control — while DAM4 has no wind recordings at all and therefore cannot be checked on this axis. This makes the SAR channels indirect *response/damping-coupled* sensors, but they cannot isolate ζ without a wind-referenced amplitude model (the `A=c·v^k` suppression ratio is that model's output and is the only channel separating both directions).
* **Honest limits**: n=5 SHM recordings per state; one campaign per state (season confound — DAM3 is a single-season contrast and the only state whose γ² sign flips between seasons); single-look γ² floor ≈ 0.04 limits DAM3 sensitivity; H2 mode in DAM3 not cleanly identifiable (weak/scattered peaks) — excluded from the comparison; DAM4 has no wind-response recordings and n=16 SAR overpasses (same as DAM6), so its γ² Welch test has little power (p=0.714 is weak evidence of no effect, not proof of it); DAM4 is drawn from the same ζ/γ²/modulation sources as DAM3/DAM6 but its campaign (`03_Healthy`/`04_DAM4_111`) covers a different season window.
